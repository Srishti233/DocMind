# Acme Corp Employee Leave and Remote Work Policy

## 1. Purpose and Scope
This policy applies to all full-time employees of Acme Corp. It sets out the rules for leave and remote work and must be followed by every manager and employee.

## 2. Annual Leave
Full-time employees are entitled to 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Up to 5 unused days may be carried over to the next year. Leave requests must be approved by the employee's manager at least 10 working days in advance.

## 3. Sick Leave
Employees receive 12 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for absences longer than 3 consecutive days.

## 4. Remote Work
### 4.1 Eligibility
Employees who have completed their 6-month probation are eligible for remote work up to 3 days per week.
### 4.2 Equipment
The company provides a laptop and a monthly internet stipend of $40 to remote employees.

## 5. Compliance
Violations of this policy may lead to disciplinary action, up to and including termination.
