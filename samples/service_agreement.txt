SERVICE AGREEMENT

This Service Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of 1 March 2025 (the "Effective Date") between BlueRiver Consulting Ltd ("Contractor") and Helios Retail Inc ("Client").

WHEREAS the Client wishes to engage the Contractor to provide software consulting services; and
WHEREAS the Contractor agrees to provide such services on the terms set out below.

1. Services
The Contractor shall provide software consulting services as described in each Statement of Work.

2. Payment
2.1 Fees. The Client shall pay the Contractor a fee of $120 per hour, invoiced monthly.
2.2 Late Payment. Invoices unpaid after 30 days incur interest at 1.5% per month.

3. Confidentiality
Each party shall keep the other party's confidential information secret for 5 years after termination of this Agreement.

4. Termination
4.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement with 60 days' written notice.
4.2 The Client may terminate immediately if the Contractor commits a material breach.

5. Liability
The Contractor's total liability under this Agreement shall not exceed $50,000.

6. Governing Law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF the parties have signed this Agreement.
